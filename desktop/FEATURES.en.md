VoidGram Desktop is built on AyuGram Desktop 7.2.9 (Telegram Desktop, Qt 6).

- **AyuGram**: ghost mode, saved deleted and edited messages, regex filters, appearance and chat settings.
- **VoidGram badges**: an Ed25519-signed registry from `badge.vinx.best`, the sponsor badge next to the name in the profile, chat and chat list.
- **Avatar frames**: animated APNG around the avatar in the profile, chat list, messages, settings and side menu; pick the frame and its size.
- **Name styles**: font, colour and glow of the name in the profile, chat and chat list; glowing reactions.
- **Bubble skins** for sponsors and a gold frame for the owner.
- **Gifts (NFT)** granted through VoidGram show in the profile's gifts tab.
- **VoidGram profile**: extra usernames, a phone number, a TON balance and a rating; change your own in your own profile when the owner allows it.
- **App icons** as in the iOS client; the choice changes the launcher icon.
- VoidGram name, logo and window id `com.voidgram.desktop`, one-command install.
